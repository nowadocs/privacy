Privacy policy

Applies to: Bastion: Tower Defense, Ruin Runner, Hashroom: Mining Tycoon, Conquer the World. Last updated: 26 November 2026.

Summary

These games do not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. They have no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, coins, settings and similar) is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

Photos (Conquer the World only)

You can choose a photo for your ruler profile. The photo is picked with the system photo picker, shrunk to a small square and saved inside the game on your device. It is never uploaded or shared. You can remove it at any time from the profile screen.

Permissions

The games may use vibration for game feedback. They do not request location, contacts, microphone or camera permissions.

Internet

The games work fully offline and do not send data over the internet.

Children

The games are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds features such as online saves, purchases or ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
